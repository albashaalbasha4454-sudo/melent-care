# MELENT CARE — Electrical Specification

## General Electrical Scope

This document defines the electrical requirements for the MELENT CARE exhibition booth.

## Main Requirements

- Hidden LED lighting inside each display cabinet.
- Backlighting or concealed lighting for the main logo area.
- Power point behind the central TV on the 5 m wall.
- Hidden LED lighting around the mirror on the 3 m wall.
- Power supply for the reception counter.
- Service socket inside or behind the booth for maintenance.
